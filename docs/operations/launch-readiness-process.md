# Launch Readiness Process

## Purpose
Ensure every onboarding reaches a minimum operational quality bar before launch.

## When to use
- Before production launch.
- After major infrastructure or workflow changes.

## Readiness checks
1. Technical connection verified.
2. Core workflows validated.
3. Roles and ownership confirmed.
4. Support path and escalation channel confirmed.
5. Risk log reviewed and accepted.

## Expected result
Launch confidence with known risks documented and assigned.

## Troubleshooting
- Missing ownership: block launch until owner assignment is complete.
- Incomplete checks: use checklists and rerun validation.
- Timeline pressure: prioritize critical readiness criteria first.
