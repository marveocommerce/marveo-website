# Support Handoff Workflow

## Purpose
Ensure onboarding transitions smoothly from setup to ongoing operations support.

## When to use
- After connector/adapter verification.
- Before go-live or pilot completion.

## Handoff steps
1. Confirm technical baseline is stable.
2. Share operational ownership map (teams + responsibilities).
3. Share known constraints and open risks.
4. Confirm support contact channels and response expectations.
5. Record handoff summary in customer record.

## Expected result
Support and customer teams share one clear operational model.

## Troubleshooting
- Missing context during handoff: use handoff template and mandatory checklist.
- Re-opened setup issues: route to onboarding queue with linked evidence.
- SLA confusion: align on response tiers before launch.

## Handoff template fields
- Workspace
- Stack type
- Connection status
- Open risks
- Escalation owner
